Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you ask for a free guide (PTE reviewer, ebook or skills assessment reviewer), we collect your first name and email address.

Why

We use them to send you the guide you asked for, and occasional emails with PTE, skills assessment, visa and life-in-Australia tips from Erick Tradie. Some emails may mention partner services (for example a registered migration agent or a PTE coaching platform). We will say so when an email includes a partner offer.

Where it is stored

Your details are stored with our email provider, Kit (kit.com), which may store data outside Australia. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

Unsubscribe anytime

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you stop receiving emails.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by replying to any of our emails.

Not advice

Our guides and emails are general information only. They are not legal, migration or financial advice. For your own case, talk to a registered migration agent (MARN).
